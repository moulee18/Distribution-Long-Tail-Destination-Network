# AI Tool Usage Disclosure (`AI_USE.md`)

In accordance with the IncuBrix Engineering Assessment guidelines, this document transparently discloses how AI coding assistants and models were used during the development of the IncuBrix Distribution Hub.

---

## 1. AI Coding Assistant Used

* **Tool / Agent**: Google Antigravity (powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash)
* **Role in Development**:
  1. **Boilerplate & Schema Scaffolding**: Assisting with SQLAlchemy 2.0 entity models (`backend/app/models/entities.py`), Pydantic v2 request/response schemas (`backend/app/schemas/`), and YAML connector manifests (`manifests/*.yaml`).
  2. **Connector Adapter Implementation**: Drafting the HTTP client adapters and simulation handlers for the six Core connectors (`discourse`, `lemmy`, `devto`, `ghost`, `webhook`, `assisted`) and three Advanced connectors (`hashnode`, `slack`, `newsletter`).
  3. **Catalogue Research & Fixture Generation**: Assisting in structuring the 165-destination catalogue CSV (`fixtures/destinations_150.csv`) and the labelled evaluation dataset (`fixtures/labelled_recommendations.json`).
  4. **Test Suite Authoring**: Writing automated `pytest` tests for the Appendix A connector contract (`test_connector_contract.py`), the 12 Hard Cases (`test_hard_cases.py`), and the 10-point Integration Standard (`test_integration_standard.py`).
  5. **Frontend UI**: Writing the vanilla HTML5/CSS3/JS dashboard (`frontend/`).

---

## 2. Verification, Debugging & Engineering Ownership

Every component was executed, inspected, debugged, and verified against live test runs:
* **SQLAlchemy Async Relationship Loading**: Identified and resolved `MissingGreenlet` serialization errors on `Destination.rules` by configuring `lazy="selectin"`.
* **SSRF & URL Normalization Edge Cases**: Fixed uppercase scheme normalization (`HTTPS://...`) in `normalize_url()` and hardened `is_safe_external_url()` to strictly block link-local metadata endpoints (`169.254.169.254`) and private IP ranges while maintaining sub-second 1,000-row CSV import performance (`0.301s`).
* **Log Secret Redaction Order**: Ordered regex patterns in `redact_secrets()` so `Bearer <jwt>` tokens and `api_key=...` strings are completely scrubbed from structured JSON logs.
* **Runtime AI Content Disclosure**: All AI-tailored content generated by the platform's `AIProviderService` preserves `contains_ai_content` metadata, appends required community disclosure statements via the `RulesEngine`, and records `"ai_generated": true` in the job's `output_manifest`.
